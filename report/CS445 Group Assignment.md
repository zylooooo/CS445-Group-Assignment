# CS445 Group Assignment

## Time Period: From Jan to Sep 2026

1. What countries are most Targeted By Ransomware Actors? **[1 Point]**
2. Why are some countries more targeted than others? **[1 Point]**
3. Which ransomware group is the most prolific or successful? **[3 Points]**
4. What is so unique about these ransomware actors' TTP that makes them so "successful"? **[3 Points]**
5. Is it common for threat actors to "re-brand" or "repurpose" older data leaks or public breaches to inflate their reputation? **[2 Points]**
6. Does victim company size or revenue puts them at higher / lower risk of attacks by ransomware actors? **[2 Points]**
7. Which Industries Are More Prone To Ransomware Threats? Why? **[3 Points]**
8. We know actors target sensitive data, but what kind of data do actors usually target? What are the kinds of data targeted in each industry? Show a breakdown comparing types of data stolen. **[5 Points]**
9. Share 3 interesting insights you observed. **[9 Points]**
10. Share lessons learnt, what were your struggles in executing the project and how did you overcome them? **[1 Point]**
